Frequently asked questions

What does the free analysis include? ↓
The free analysis gives you an overall SEO score (0–100), an AI search readiness score, and your top 3 most critical issues — all within about 15 seconds. No account or credit card required.
What's included in the full $199 report? ↓
The full report includes a complete prioritised list of all issues found, a competitor comparison against 3 similar websites, and a detailed 90-day action plan covering both traditional SEO and AI search optimisation. You receive an interactive on-screen report plus a downloadable PDF.
What is AI search optimisation? ↓
AI search optimisation ensures your content is structured so that AI assistants (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google's AI Overviews, Bing AI, Claude, Gemini) cite and recommend your website. As more searches happen through AI interfaces, this is becoming as important as traditional Google SEO.
How does the monthly subscription work? ↓
For $49/month, we run a full audit of your website every month, track your score progress, and update your action plan based on what's been fixed and what's changed. You can cancel anytime from your email — no questions asked.
How accurate is the analysis? ↓
Our analysis is powered by Claude (Anthropic's AI) and examines your actual page content. While we don't have access to Google Search Console data, our technical checks, content analysis, and AI readiness assessment are highly actionable. Most clients see measurable improvements within 30–60 days.
Is my data secure? ↓
Payments are processed by Stripe — we never see your card details. Your email and audit results are stored securely in Supabase (a SOC 2 compliant platform). We don't share your data with third parties.
